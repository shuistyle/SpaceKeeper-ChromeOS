    ChromeOS doesn't let apps see every desk at once, so SpaceKeeper learns them as you use them. Visit any desk that's missing (Search + ]) and it will appear here.

    Settings and help

    Using SpaceKeeper

    • Click a desk (or press Enter) to go to it. 1–9 jump to the first nine.
    • Double-click (or press F2 or R) to name it. Enter saves, Esc cancels.
    • Right-click (or press Shift + F10, or M) for colour, moving and removing.
    • Drag desks, or press Alt + Shift + arrow keys, to put them in your order.
    • Ctrl + Alt + N adds a desk.

    Names and colours are SpaceKeeper's own labels, saved only in this browser. ChromeOS doesn't let apps rename desks or change their order in Overview.
